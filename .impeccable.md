# Design Context — AlphaZero Learning Academy

## Product Context
- **Product:** AlphaZero Learning Academy (Multi-tenant SaaS e-learning platform)
- **Target Audience:** High school and university students, teachers, content producers, and academic institutions in the MENA region (optimized for low-bandwidth environments).
- **Primary Use Cases:**
  1. High-performance HLS video learning with RSA device locking.
  2. Composite lessons with interleaved text, LaTeX math formulas, and MCQ checkpoints.
  3. Physical library scratch code redemption for course unlocks.
  4. Real-time bitmask progress tracking (`VARBIT`).
  5. Teacher handwritten exam grading and verified Q&A discussions.
- **Brand Tone & Personality:** Minimal, Utilitarian, Serious, Fast, and Professional. Avoids decorative fluff and AI clichés; delivers an ultra-crisp, enterprise-grade feel.

## Visual Design Tokens
- **Typography:**
  - Latin Display & Body: `Outfit` (400, 500, 600, 700)
  - Arabic Display & Body: `Cairo` (400, 600, 700)
  - Code, Math & Data: `JetBrains Mono` (400, 500, 700)
- **Color Palette (Restrained & High-Contrast):**
  - Primary / Text: `#111827` (Near-Black)
  - Canvas: `#FAFAFA`
  - Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Subtle Borders: `#E5E7EB`
  - Accent / Tenant Base: `#0F172A` (Slate-Black)
  - Semantic Success: `#10B981` (Emerald)
  - Semantic Warning: `#F59E0B` (Amber)
  - Semantic Error: `#EF4444` (Crimson)
  - Semantic Info: `#3B82F6` (Blue)
- **Spacing Scale:** 8px base rhythm (`2xs: 2px`, `xs: 4px`, `sm: 8px`, `md: 16px`, `lg: 24px`, `xl: 32px`, `2xl: 48px`).
- **Motion:** Functional micro-transitions (150ms ease-out). No bouncy animations.
