## 1. Telegram & Facebook Community Mining (Teacher & Student Voice)

### The Teacher Sentiment: Frustration & Betrayal
* **The "Telegram Leak" Crisis:**
  * Teachers view Telegram channels as blatant thieves who profit from their name while contributing nothing.
  * *Verbatim sentiment from Syrian teacher groups:*
    > *"صورت الدورة بمعهد بالشام وكلفتني وقت وتعب، بعد يومين لقيت رابط القناة على تيليغرام عم يبيعوا الرابط بـ 20 ألف ليرة! هيك الأساتذة رح يبطلوا يعطوا أونلاين بالمرة."*  
    > *(I filmed the course at an institute in Damascus and it cost me time and effort. Two days later, I found a Telegram channel selling the link for 20k SYP! This is why teachers will stop teaching online altogether.)*
* **The "Device & Name Watermark" Demand:**
  * Teachers are actively asking if there is a system that burns the student's name or phone number into the video so that whoever screen-records the lesson exposes their identity and can be legally or academically expelled from the course.

### The Student Sentiment: Desperation for Affordable Focus
* **The Pressure of National Exams:**
  * In Syria, Baccalaureate exam scores determine university acceptance into high-status fields (Medicine, Engineering, Pharmacy).
  * Students are desperate for top Damascus and Aleppo teachers, but living in provincial towns (Hama, Daraa, Tartus, rural Aleppo) makes traveling to metropolitan institutes impossible.
  * *Verbatim student sentiment:*
    > *"بدنا بس مادة الرياضيات للأستاذ الفلاني، ما بدي اشتري المنهاج كامل من التطبيقات بـ 4 ملايين، خلونا نشتري مواد مفردة!"*  
    > *(We just want math from that specific teacher. I don't want to buy the whole curriculum from apps for 4 million pounds. Let us buy individual subjects!)*

## 2. Competitive Language Map

| Concept | What Competitors Say | What Customers Actually Say (Voice of Customer) | What Plexor Should Say |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Course Access** | "اشترك بالباقة السنوية الكاملة" (Subscribe to annual full pack) | "بدي بس مادة الرياضيات / وحدة الكهرباء" (I just want Math / Electricity unit) | **"اشترك بالمادة أو الوحدة يلي بتلزمك بس" (Subscribe only to the subject or unit you need)** |
| **Security** | "محتوى حصري ومحمي" (Exclusive protected content) | "عم يتسرب ع التيلغرام" (It's leaking on Telegram) | **"حماية كاملة من التسريب بعلامة مائية ديناميكية" (Complete leak protection with dynamic watermarking)** |
| **Payment** | "تحويل بنكي / دفع إلكتروني" (Bank transfer / electronic) | "معي كاش بالمكتبة أو كاش سيريتل/شام كاش" (I have cash at the library or Syriatel/Sham Cash) | **"كود فوري من أقرب مكتبة أو دفع مباشر بشام كاش" (Instant code from nearest library or direct Sham Cash)** |
| **Grading** | "اختبر معلوماتك باختبارات مؤتمتة" (Test yourself with MCQs) | "بدي الأستاذ يصلحلي ورقة الفحص ويقلي وين خطأت" (I want the teacher to grade my paper and tell me where I failed) | **"تصحيح يدوي لورقة الامتحان بخط إيدك من الأستاذ نفسه" (Manual grading of your handwritten exam by the teacher himself)** |
