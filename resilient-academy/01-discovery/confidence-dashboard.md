## 3. Highest Confidence Findings (Solid Ground)
1. **The Market Need is Real & Urgent:** Shadow education in Syria is not an elective luxury; it is a life-or-death requirement for university entry. Over 250,000 students take the Baccalaureate annually, and their families spend millions of pounds on tutoring.
2. **The Offline Code + Sham Cash Model is Proven:** Incumbents like Labib have already demonstrated that retail library scratch cards and Sham Cash are the winning monetization mechanisms in Syria.
3. **The Infrastructure Unit Economics Work:** Using Cloudflare R2 and AWS Lambda FFmpeg reduces bandwidth costs to essentially zero, removing the biggest barrier for a solo founder.

---

## 4. Lowest Confidence Findings & Critical Unknowns (Thin Ice)
1. **Teacher Commitment to Exclusive Hosting:** While teachers hate piracy, will they actually stop posting on free Telegram channels and require their students to register on Plexor?
2. **Retail Library Stocking Friction:** Will independent stationery owners readily accept printing or holding Plexor scratch codes, or will they demand upfront payment?
3. **Device Binding vs. Student Sharing:** Can simple device fingerprints sufficiently deter credential sharing without creating false-positive lockouts when students get a new phone?

---

## 5. Immediate Verification Actions for Founder
* **Action 1 (Star Teacher Commitment):** Approach 2 high-reputation teachers with a mock agreement: *"I will build your custom branded academy and protect your lessons with DRM; will you commit your next revision unit to Plexor for a 15% fee?"*
* **Action 2 (Library Partnership Test):** Visit 2 copy centers/libraries near major Damascus high schools or institutes and ask the owner: *"If an academy issues course codes through my app, will you sell them to students for a 10% commission on cash collected?"*
