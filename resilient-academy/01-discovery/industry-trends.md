## 2. Technology Shifts & Adoption Curves

```
Early Adoption ───────► Growth / Mainstream ───────► Maturity
      │                         │                         │
[Full Widevine DRM]    [HLS Adaptive Streaming]     [Basic MP4 Video]
[AI Handwritten OCR]   [Mobile Wallets/Sham Cash]   [Paper Scratch Cards]
```

* **Adaptive Bitrate Streaming over HTTP/3:** Essential for high-packet-loss mobile networks. Chunked HLS allows students on fluctuating 3G connections to maintain audio/video sync without constant buffering.
* **Forensic Dynamic Watermarking:** Modern video players overlay authenticated student identifiers (phone number, dynamic timestamp) directly on top of the video canvas. If a student attempts to record their screen or film with another phone, their identity is stamped indelibly across the leak, practically eliminating unauthorized sharing.
