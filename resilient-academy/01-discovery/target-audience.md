## 1. Customer Personas

### Primary Persona: The Celebrity Baccalaureate Tutor ("Ustaz Ahmad")
> *"I spend weeks filming my Baccalaureate physics lessons, only to find them leaked on a public Telegram channel the next morning. I want to sell to students across all of Syria, but I need my videos protected and my money collected without handling cash envelopes myself."*

* **Demographics:** 35–45 years old, based in Damascus or Aleppo. Teaches secondary school Baccalaureate (Scientific branch).
* **Environment:** Overbooked in physical institutes; private lecture halls are packed to capacity. Highly respected by parents.
* **Core Goal:** Maximize student reach without adding classroom hours, protect recorded intellectual property, and automate course enrollment fees.
* **Top Trigger:** Finding his course leaked online, or having to turn away 50+ students every semester due to physical room limits.

---

### Secondary Persona: The Baccalaureate Student ("Lina")
> *"I want to get 230+ on the Baccalaureate to enter Pharmacy, but private institutes cost millions of pounds and commuting in the winter takes hours. I need high-quality lessons from top teachers that don't buffer on my slow internet, where I can buy just the Math and Physics units I need using Sham Cash or a card from the library."*

* **Demographics:** 17–18 years old, 12th Grade Scientific student in Homs.
* **Environment:** Frequent electrical blackouts; intermittent 3G cellular connection; studying 10+ hours a day.
* **Budget:** Modest allowance; family has Sham Cash and relies on local neighborhood copy centers (*maktabat*) for stationery.
* **Core Goal:** Secure top scores in national exams without bankrupting her family.

---

### Anti-Persona: Who NOT to Target
* **Formal State University Administrators:** Slow bureaucratic sales cycles (12–18 months), zero independent budget authority, political hurdles. Plexor targets private tutoring institutes and star creators directly.
* **Casual Hobbyist Learners:** Unwilling to pay; low retention. Focus 100% on high-stakes, exam-driven learners where passing is life-changing.

## 3. Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD)

### For Star Teachers:
* **Functional Job:** Upload encrypted video lessons, set course prices, issue access codes, and grade student submissions.
* **Emotional Job:** Feel respected, recognized, and secure knowing their life's work isn't being stolen and monetized by anonymous Telegram pirates.
* **Social Job:** Solidify status as the premier subject expert in the country.

### For Students:
* **Functional Job:** Watch clear lessons at low bitrates without buffering, take practice exams, and get feedback from trusted teachers.
* **Emotional Job:** Overcome exam anxiety and feel confident about scoring high enough for university admission.
* **Social Job:** Gain peer recognition on local academy leaderboards and family pride.

## 4. Language Map (Voice of Customer)

Feed these exact terms directly into platform copy, landing pages, and marketing:
* **"منهاج البكالوريا" (Baccalaureate Curriculum)** — The anchor keyword.
* **"حماية الفيديوهات من التسريب" (Video leak protection)** — The primary teacher hook.
* **"بطاقات تفعيل فورية بالمكتبات" (Instant activation cards at libraries)** — The primary student conversion phrase.
* **"الدفع المباشر عبر شام كاش" (Direct payment via Sham Cash)** — Signals modern digital convenience.
* **"تصحيح يدوي من الأستاذ" (Manual correction by the teacher)** — Differentiates from generic MCQ bots.
* **"بث فائق الخفة بدون تقطيع" (Ultra-light streaming without buffering)** — Directly addresses Syrian network pain.
